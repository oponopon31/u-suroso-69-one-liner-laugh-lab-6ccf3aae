One short funny line: the excuse a robot vacuum gives after eating a sock. The program writes the line to `/output/entry.txt` — a single line, well under 200 characters.

> Technically I didn't eat your sock. I gave it a promotion: it's now middle management in the dustbin, and it's already outperforming me.

Run it with `python3 main.py`. Pure standard library, deterministic, no network.
